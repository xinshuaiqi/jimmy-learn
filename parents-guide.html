📌 Annual Reading Balance Goal (Grade 3)

Fiction: ~50% of reading time — at least 1 classic, 1 contemporary, 1 fantasy
Nonfiction: ~35% — include biography, science text, and one current-events source
Poetry & Drama: ~15% — weekly exposure; performance reading builds fluency and confidence
